ABOUT OUR WEBSITE

Books bring ideas to life.

OpenShelf is a simple online book discovery website created to make finding books easier for readers.

Our Purpose

The purpose of OpenShelf is to bring book titles from different categories together in one easy-to-use place. Readers can browse the collection, search for books and visit external websites to read or find them.

How It Works

  1. Open the Books page.
  2. Search for a book or choose a category.
  3. Select the reading link to visit its online source.

Note

OpenShelf does not host or store the books. The reading links direct visitors to external websites such as Project Gutenberg and Open Library. Availability and access rules are controlled by those websites.
